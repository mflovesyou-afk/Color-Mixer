1. The Law of Neutralization (Opposing Tones)

Colors directly opposite each other on the color wheel cancel each other out to create a neutral tone.

  • Yellow + Violet = Neutral Beige/Platinum
  • Orange + Blue = Neutral Brown/Ash Blonde
  • Red + Green = Neutral Brunette
